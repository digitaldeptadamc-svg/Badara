Badara
Botanical Name :

  • Ziziphus mauritiana

    • Family Name :

  • Rhamnaceae

    • Local Name :

  • Badara / Ber

    • Vernacular Name :

  • Hindi – Ber

  • English – Indian Jujube

    • Synonyms :

  • Ziziphus jujuba

  • Ziziphus indica


    • Habitat :

  • Grows in tropical and subtropical regions

  • Common in dry and arid areas of India

  • Tolerates poor soils and drought

  • Small to medium-sized thorny tree

  • Leaves: Simple, ovate, shiny green

  • Flowers: Small, greenish-yellow

  • Fruits: Round/oval, green turning reddish-brown when ripe

  • Bark: Dark grey, rough

  • Leaves

  • Fruits

  • Seeds

  • Tannins


    • Actions :

  • Anti-inflammatory

  • Antimicrobial

  • Sedative


    • Properties :

  • Boosts immunity

  • Improves digestion

  • Calming effect

  • Nourishing


    • Indications :

  • Weakness and fatigue

  • Digestive disorders

  • Skin diseases

  • Insomnia

  • Cough and cold
